# opennoir

The main journey is **Marketplace → My team → Work → Earnings**.

- Home: team health, modeled service value, attention items and shortcuts.
- Marketplace: robots and AI agents; company, capability and price filters; comparison; product review; demo checkout; purchases added to My team.
- My team: owned/connected robots and AI agents, IDs, health, history. Add marketplace purchases, custom AI, custom robots, or pair by ID and code.
- Work: explore sample opportunities, capability-matched recommendations, review requirements/duration/estimated gross, choose a team member and connected client. Manage ongoing, paused and completed work. Task attempts live in Activity.
- Earnings: recorded gross, costs, modeled 5% fee, net; team breakdown, transaction list, demo balance and simulated payout history. Job estimates are not credited to the balance.
- Activity: each run is one attempt at a task; start, simulate outcome, retry and inspect history.
- People & businesses: ID lookup, profile preview, connect and view scoped activity.
- Reports: deeper usage/service economics and CSV export.
- Issues & maintenance: open/resolved issues, recorded history, guided simulated resolution.
- Notifications: separate header bell for workspace updates and mark-read state.
- Assistant: saved scripted guidance, not live AI.
- Help & support: saved cases with screenshots or an explicitly chosen screen capture.
- Settings: gradients, motion, workspace, advanced people filters, notifications, demo payment/payout preferences and visual-work privacy.
- My account: display name, local opennoir ID, security/sign-in status and guide.
- Feedback: persistent page pins, all-page review, replies, resolve/reopen and export.

## Confidential work
The console masks client/task names in work cards and activity listings and blocks visual work previews for confidential jobs. Global privacy settings can hide all work previews. This is display masking for the trusted local operator, not server-side permissions or NDA enforcement; raw local records and exports remain operator-accessible.

## Local demo boundaries
SQLite persistence, comments, pairing and screenshots are real local records. Purchases, opportunities, execution, earnings, fees, payouts and work views are simulated. No real seller inventory, public sign-in, payment processing, bank integration, device control, live video or AI provider is connected. Company names in reference concepts do not indicate affiliation or official offers.

## Team map and companion rail

My team includes an offline schematic map with sample robot positions and an explicitly unowned robotaxi preview. Select pins for details; confidential active work has no displayed pin. No geolocation, map provider, vehicle booking or telemetry is connected. The right-side assistant starts minimized, expands on request, and shares the saved scripted conversation with the Assistant page. Core sidebar items now have visible labels, including Earnings and My team.

The team explorer uses a large map on the left and a scrollable robot-results panel on the right. Results show concept photos, status, sample area, health and battery. Cards and pins share selection, and search/status filters apply to both. AI agents appear separately below or under the AI agents filter. On narrow screens the results stack below the map.
